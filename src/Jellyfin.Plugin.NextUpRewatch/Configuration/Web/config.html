NextUp Rewatch Configuration

Intelligently manages the Jellyfin "Next Up" carousel when the client-side "Enable Rewatching in Next Up" setting is enabled. It filters out stalled completed shows and dead-end specials while preserving active rewatching sequences.

Automatically hide completed series that stay stuck in "Next Up" with their final watched episode, while preserving active rewatches.
Automatically enables rewatching for all clients (including Moonfin, Swiftfin, Infuse) by injecting enableRewatching=true on Next Up requests, even if the client app lacks a rewatching toggle.
Updates the LastPlayedDate of active rewatch episodes to match the series' latest watch timestamp. This ensures clients (like Moonfin) sort them at the top of "Continue Watching" instead of sinking them by their original watch date.
Log detailed filtering decisions in the Jellyfin server log.
